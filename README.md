# The Unknown Archive

> **Explore the unknown. Separate belief from evidence. Follow the question.**

**The Unknown Archive** is an interactive web archive exploring the strange borderlands between **religion, mythology, science, psychology, folklore, paranormal claims, and internet culture**.

It is designed around one simple principle:

**Interesting does not automatically mean true.**

Every topic is classified according to its level of support, allowing readers to explore unusual ideas without blurring the line between documented tradition, scientific evidence, speculation, folklore, and fiction.

## 🧭 Evidence Classification

Every major entry receives a status badge:

| Status                         | Meaning                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🟢 **Established**             | Supported by strong evidence or well-established knowledge |
| 🔵 **Documented Tradition**    | Historically or religiously documented belief              |
| 🟡 **Disputed**                | Experts, interpretations, or evidence disagree             |
| 🟠 **Speculative**             | Interesting hypothesis with insufficient evidence          |
| 🟣 **Folklore**                | Cultural stories, traditions, and mythology                |
| 🔴 **Fiction / Internet Lore** | Modern fictional or internet-origin material               |

This classification is a core part of the project.

For example, the archive explicitly distinguishes between **unidentified phenomena and evidence for extraterrestrial life**, and between **extra dimensions in theoretical physics and mystical "planes."**

## 📚 Research Philosophy

The archive is not intended to prove or disprove every subject it contains.

Instead, it asks:

* What is actually documented?
* What is supported by evidence?
* What is disputed?
* What is speculation?
* What originated as folklore?
* What is explicitly fictional?
* Where does the evidence stop?
* What questions remain unanswered?

Each entry attempts to separate:

**Known → Unknown → Interpretation → Misconception**

rather than presenting every extraordinary claim as fact.

---

## ⚠️ Research Disclaimer

The current archive was assembled from uploaded research reports and their cited material.

The website itself explicitly states that its claims are **not independently verified within the application**.

Some entries contain:

* scholarly interpretations
* religious interpretations
* historical claims
* folklore
* speculative hypotheses
* disputed information
* internet-origin material

These categories should not be treated as equivalent.

Where the underlying reports disagree, the archive attempts to preserve that disagreement instead of silently choosing one version.

## 🗺️ Current Limitations

Some subjects originally considered for the wider archive are not yet developed because the available research material does not sufficiently cover them.

Examples include:

* Near-death experiences
* Dreams
* Déjà vu
* Lost civilisations
* Additional afterlife concepts
* Other paranormal and esoteric subjects

The project intentionally avoids inventing research where sources are missing.

## 🤝 Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially contributions that improve **accuracy and sourcing**.

Before submitting a contribution:

1. Provide reliable sources where possible.
2. Separate fact from interpretation.
3. Clearly label speculation.
4. Do not present folklore as scientific evidence.
5. Do not present fictional internet lore as a documented phenomenon.
6. Preserve disagreements between credible sources.
7. Avoid sensationalizing uncertain claims.

A contribution that says **"we don't know"** is completely valid.
